Furry Friends
Furry Friends Training and Care is a dog training/care and cat care centre in Co. Wicklow. It has been in business for 25 years and holds a five-star Google rating from 1,200 reviews. This brief is the client context for Parts 2, 3 and 4.
What Furry Friends offers
•	Dog training: good-behaviour classes, socialisation with other dogs, and rehabilitation for dogs from traumatic backgrounds.
•	Cat care: a climbing jungle, indoor and outdoor, and cat day care.
•	Doggy day care for all breeds, with pick-up and drop-off.
•	Grooming and spa for dogs and cats, including a heated swimming pool for dogs.
•	New > the Doggy Hotel: heated private rooms for dogs and cats for overnight and weekend stays.

What the client wants
1.	More bookings for grooming and spa, training, and the new Doggy Hotel.
2.	More brand awareness, and more traffic to its social media channels

What the client wants people to find online
●	All services, with a clear breakdown of fees.
●	The new Doggy Hotel and its heated private rooms.
●	Testimonials from happy customers.
●	Tips on encouraging good behaviour in dogs and cats at home

Your client update card
In your lab in the week of 12 October you will draw a client update card. It adds one real-world change to the brief, for example a new location, a new service, a target audience shift or a seasonal promotion. Your card is unique to you and is recorded against your name. Your card must show up in your scope document, your website and your social media campaign. Work that ignores your card, or uses someone else's, loses the marks for meeting the brief.
