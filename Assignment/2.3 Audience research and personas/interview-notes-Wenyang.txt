4 days in office, 1 from home. During the weekend usually stay at home.

3. Tell me about your pet or pets. What are they, how old, and how long have you had them?
How did they come to you: a breeder, a rescue, a friend?
Behaviours: what pets they have
2 british short hair cats, Pepe and Bonnie. had them for a year and two months, they are a year and a half. Rescued.

4. Walk me through a typical day with your pet, from morning to night.
Who walks, feeds and plays with them? What do you do together at weekends?
Behaviours: what they do with their pets
Wake up when they come to lick her hair so she gets up and goes to have breakfast. After they play a bit she feeds them and leaves the house to go to work. Comes back 9 hours later and play a bit with the cats, and feed them again before having dinner and going to bed.

5. How would you describe the role your pet plays in your life?
Can you give me an example of a time that shows that?
Behaviours: the role pets play in their life
They are like a less demanding child, they require less effort but a strong emotional bond that she can't leave without them. After adopting she felt like this, but not immediately at the very beginning, after she started to see how much they depended on her and she began to form this bond. She felt responsible for their safety.

Did you search online, ask someone, or see it on social media? Which app or site? Did you book online, by phone or in person?
Behaviours: how they find and book services (also useful for Part 4)
Never paid for any service like grooming or care/sitter. Paid for medicine and she got it from the vet, she booked the slot in the website, it had a calendar that allowed her to pick a time and choose the service that she needed from the vet, she found it easy to use so she preferred it over calling.

7. Which social media apps do you use most, and do you follow any pet accounts or pet businesses?
What kind of pet posts make you stop scrolling?
Behaviours: platforms and content they respond to (also useful for Part 4)
Instagram. She follows some pets influencers but no businesses. She likes the AI posts when cats are singing or dancing, or when a TV show is re-made with cats instead of humans.

8. What's the hardest part of looking after your pet: grooming, training, or day-to-day care?
Tell me about a specific time that was difficult. What did you do?
Pain points: grooming, training, care
Cleaning is the hardest part, they shed often so she needs to vacuum often. Furniture can look covered by hair. Cleaning their automatic litter box needs some disassembling and cleaning which is complicated.

9. What do you do with your pet when you go away overnight or on holiday?
How do you feel about leaving them? Has anything ever gone wrong?
Pain points: links to the Doggy Hotel
She asks a friend to take care of them. She is very worried at the same time cause she doesn't want them to be treated differently as when they are with her. Nothing went wrong.

10. Have you ever had a bad experience with a pet care business, or decided not to use one? What happened?
What would have changed your mind?
Pain points: what stops them booking
No issues.

11. If you were choosing somewhere new to mind your pet, what would you need to see or know before you'd trust them?
Prices? Photos of the place? Reviews? Being able to visit first?
Pain points: what the website needs to show
So many things. Check how many pets they host simultaneously and where they stay (free or in a cage). Check if there are other animals. Check if they have people looking after them 24/7 and if they are suitable to take care of the cats needs or if they have some vet training and how much it costs. Needs to know what they'll be fed. Reviews are important and she'd want to see the place first.

12. What is a challenge that you faced while rising them?
Understanding cat nutrition. Spent a lot of time searching for what would be best for their diet. Trying to balance great quality with affordability. Having a good balance between dry and wet food.
