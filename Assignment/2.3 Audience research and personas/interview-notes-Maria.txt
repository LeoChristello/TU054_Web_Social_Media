Interview - Maria

1. Can you tell me a bit about yourself: roughly what age bracket you're in, where you live, and what you do for work?
Demographics: 55-64, Argentina, Full Time Employee.

2. What does a normal working week look like for you? How many hours are you out of the house? Do you work from home on any days? Does anyone else live with you?
Demographics and behaviours: shows whether day care or pick-up would matter
4 days work from home, 1 in office. Weekends sometimes in sometimes out.

3. Tell me about your pet or pets. What are they, how old, and how long have you had them?
How did they come to you: a breeder, a rescue, a friend?
Behaviours: what pets they have
Dog. Bonnie. 8 years old, had for 7 years. She helped a friend to adopt it, the friend had to give the dog up and she took it in.

4. Walk me through a typical day with your pet, from morning to night.
Who walks, feeds and plays with them? What do you do together at weekends?
Behaviours: what they do with their pets
Food in the morning, walk outside twice a day or sometimes three. She takes care of the dog. Keeps her company during working from home or the weekends.

5. How would you describe the role your pet plays in your life?
Can you give me an example of a time that shows that?
Behaviours: the role pets play in their life
Company. When she works from home she is always keeping her company and also sleeps next to her. Or when she is not home and comes back, she is waiting behind the door happy to see her.

6. Think about the last time you paid for any pet service: grooming, training, day care, kennels or a sitter. How did you find it, and how did you book it?
Did you search online, ask someone, or see it on social media? Which app or site? Did you book online, by phone or in person?
Behaviours: how they find and book services (also useful for Part 4)
Last time she travelled, she paid for a kennel to take the dog in. A friend recommended her the place. Her friend knew someone who works there. She gave her the whatsapp number and she arranged everything messaging via whatsapp.

7. Which social media apps do you use most, and do you follow any pet accounts or pet businesses?
What kind of pet posts make you stop scrolling?
Behaviours: platforms and content they respond to (also useful for Part 4)
Whatsapp, Instagram, Facebook in that order. She follows many, she stops when she sees videos about the pets cause they are doing funny things or they showcase abilities.

8. What's the hardest part of looking after your pet: grooming, training, or day-to-day care?
Tell me about a specific time that was difficult. What did you do?
Pain points: grooming, training, care
Showering the dog is the most complicated part because the dog has reduced mobility. She needs to bring the dog upstairs to the bathtub and it is challenging.

9. What do you do with your pet when you go away overnight or on holiday?
How do you feel about leaving them? Has anything ever gone wrong?
Pain points: links to the Doggy Hotel
A couple of times brought to the kennel, others a family member would take care. Never had issues.

10. Have you ever had a bad experience with a pet care business, or decided not to use one? What happened?
What would have changed your mind?
Pain points: what stops them booking
No issues.

11. If you were choosing somewhere new to mind your pet, what would you need to see or know before you'd trust them?
Prices? Photos of the place? Reviews? Being able to visit first?
Pain points: what the website needs to show
She would look for places that trusted friends recommend and that the places looks nice and ideally at a convenient location. If it is to leave the dog for many days then she prefers a big place and she would like to see it beforehand.

12. What is a challenge that you faced while rasing them?
4 years ago the dog began having trouble walking, she took her to the vet and after that to the hospital because the vet was significantly more expensive and also the hospital provides better coverage. They provided a wheeled device so that the dog could continue to walk. She now continues to regularly check on her legs and ensures Bonnie gets all the needed medical attention.
