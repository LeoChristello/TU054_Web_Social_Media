1.	Footer (Step 14): What happened when you deleted flex: 1? What do you think it does?
Deleting it caused the footer to start right after 'main' ended, leaving a significant amount of white space after it. I played around with it and noticed it can have a functional value between 0 and 1, whereas 1 would cause it to stick to the bottom of the page, and other values such as 0.5 would cause it to stay vertically centered between the end of 'main' and the bottom of the page.

My navbar and my footer. The navbar in the mobile version is opened and closed, and the footer displays less information.
